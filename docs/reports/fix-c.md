## 3. Findings

**(1) The expander is now labelled for a reading its rows no longer show.** `t5_depth` hangs under
`metrics.promo_depth.readings.from_price_pair.n` = 95 and its heading is the string
`t5.reading.from_price_pair` — «the price-pair reading». Its rows now print the badge. Nothing in
the build can see this: the population is unchanged (95 rows still have an arithmetic depth), the
convergence check does not read labels, and the poisoned-cell guard tests figures. The good news is
that it is only a label problem — **all 95 price-pair rows carry a printed badge**, so not one row
of that expander goes blank. Fixing it means a new heading in `config/ui_strings.yaml`, and this
contract says no other surface moves; it is an operator ruling, the same shape as fix-b's (1).

**(2) The validate pack prints both readings and the old price itself — and that is not a promo
surface.** `scripts/build_validate_pack.py` renders per position row `depth.from_printed_badge`,
`depth.from_price_pair` and «старая цена (вход глубины, ПОМЕЧЕН)» in as many words. It is the pack
the operator ruled on the extraction WITH, at the 5c2-validate sitting, and its record is sealed and
pinned. 3.22 (1) binds «row-level promo SURFACES»; a review artifact whose purpose is to show the
operator what was extracted is the instrument, not the surface. Named here so the reading is on
record rather than assumed, and untouched.

**This rules on the artifact, not on the producer.** `results/validate_5c2_pack.json` is sealed and
is never regenerated; `scripts/build_validate_pack.py` is live code, and the next person who runs it
gets a fresh per-row rendering carrying `depth.from_price_pair` beside the promo price. Whether the
PRODUCER should keep doing that is a separate ruling and is not made here.

**(3) The badge is invertible too — just not to the number the law names.** `promo / (1 − badge)`
on the 15 drawn rows that have an old price returns it with a median error of **0.49 ₴** and a
maximum of **8.91 ₴**, and on **1 of the 15** it still lands on `price_old` to the kopiyka, because
that chain's printed percentage happens to equal the arithmetic. What changed is whose number it
is: the badge is the chain's own claim, printed on its own leaflet, and anything derived from it is
the chain's arithmetic. The extracted old price — our model's reading of a crossed-out figure — no
longer reaches a row. That is what 3.22 (1) asks for, and the residual is worth stating out loud
rather than leaving for someone to rediscover.

**(4) The export's `law` field points at a finding 3.22 has since closed.**
`promo.positions_table.law` ends «see `docs/reports/fix-b.md` finding (1)», which is still true and
now sends a reader to a question that has an answer. Nothing to do here: the export is byte-identical
by design this contract, and rewording it would move an artifact for prose. A one-line follow-up for
whichever contract next regenerates the export.

**(5) A control that decays on every landing.** See Dv374. The intruder name in
`test_a_twelfth_marked_block_is_refused_rather_than_stripped` has to move every time an amendment
lands, and the suite has never forced it — a duplicate name refuses too, so the test stays green
while testing a different thing. It was left behind once already, in fix-a. It now carries
`assert intruder not in writer.BLOCKS_TODAY`, which makes the next omission red. The class is worth
a look at the sitting: a negative control whose validity depends on a fact the suite does not check
is a control with a shelf life.
