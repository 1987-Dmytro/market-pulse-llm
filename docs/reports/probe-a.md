## 1. D1 ($0) — `reader_thread_gm4`, registered before anything could serve it

`4cbbd24 feat(probe-a D1): the thread reader, registered before anything can serve it`

The prompt joins the fifteen already in `prompts.PROMPTS` with its own sha
`b272115637f784ad…` (`prompts.prompt_sha256("reader_thread_gm4")`, pinned in the registration). Built from
the design authority and not from taste:

- **Frame** — PRODUCT.md's seven director questions, spelled out in the prompt: a signal is only
  what answers one of them, and a thread that carries none is a normal answer with an empty list.
  (Seven, not the heading's six — the table gained question 7 on 2026-08-10. Dv390.)
- **Duties in order** (plan §2 C, the operator's clarification of 15.08) — (1) the thread, the post
  sets the topic; (2) every trade mark and chain name resolved from its context, one phrase and the
  quote it was read in; (3) only then signals. Written as three numbered duties with «never begin
  one before the one above it is finished», because the failure it guards is the one the manual
  reading did not make: a signal attached to a name nobody resolved.
- **Taxonomy exactly as ratified 15.08** — `молочный_бренд · сеть_ритейлер · категория_личное ·
  не_наш_рынок`, `спрос · жалоба · похвала · привычка · тренд` (open, with `proposed: true` as its
  only escape), `плюс_спам · скам · оффтоп`. Russian tokens inside an English prompt on purpose:
  they are the operator's ratified words, and translating them would make the prompt, the parser and
  the sitting three different taxonomies.
- **Aspects and stances are the repo's own** — `scorer.INTENTS_V2` (taste · price · packaging ·
  quality · availability · service) and `SENTIMENT_LABELS`. Not a choice: PRODUCT.md question 2
  answers itself with exactly those six words in Russian, and plan §3's last paragraph requires the
  stance layer to be comparable with the heads the model already has — «расхождения двух слоёв —
  отдельная колонка честности» is impossible between two vocabularies.
- **The four entity cases of plan §5 (4) are deliberately absent from the text.** They are the bar.
  What the prompt carries is the law they were ruled from, including «a word that is both a name and
  an ordinary word of the language is a name only where the text uses it as one» — the rule the
  reader has to apply on its own.
- **No shape line and no worked example**, unlike the position prompts: every object's keys are
  spelled out inline, so an illustration would add only values to copy.

**One parser, in `prompts.py`, as the contract asks.** `parse_reply` gains a `reader_thread_gm4`
branch after `_object` (which already unwraps a fence and reads from the first brace); the positions
prompts stay refused by name because their parser validates a tier ladder that means nothing here.
The branch is strict about DOMAINS and silent about consistency: a `subject_type` outside the
taxonomy is an answer nothing downstream can read, while a comment appearing in both `per_comment`
and `noise` is a bookkeeping slip whose two rows are each readable — and a refusal costs the whole
thread, which the probe pays for once. `stance` and `subject_id` are optional-and-nullable because
plan §3's own second signal carries neither, and an absent key read as `null` says «not stated»,
which is a different answer from «neutral».

**Both ceilings are measured, not chosen.**

| ceiling | value | how it was set |
|---|---|---|
| input | 40 000 chars, LOUD refusal | the largest of the 111 threads renders to **27 593** chars (125 payable comments, @matusi_ukr #22058; 10 784 input tokens) through this very function; median 6 095. The guard sits ~45% above the measured maximum so it cannot fire on a thread the probe is registered to read |
| output | 2 000 tokens | arithmetic on the pinned tokenizer, part by part: thread block + both summaries 98 tokens, an entity 63, a signal with its quote 103, a `per_comment` row 47, a noise row 22. A 13-payable thread (the population's 90th percentile) with three entities, two signals and a row per comment is ~1 114 |

The output ceiling does **not** cover the three threads above a hundred payable comments (125, 108,
105); they are named in the registration, and a reply that hits the ceiling comes back
`finish_reason: length` and is counted as a parse failure by cause — never read as a thread with
nothing in it.

**Three records pin this module's bytes and none of them is re-pinned** (Dv380).

### 5.6 What the three verdicts say

They were refused by the parser and they are not bad readings — which is the finding. Coerced
offline (a measurement, not a fix: the instrument froze when the endpoint appeared), **two of the
three parse whole**:

- **@mandziak:3701** — a popcorn thread. Empty entities, empty signals, empty per_comment, one

- **@VARUS_channel:10451** — a cake advertisement. VARUS resolved as `сеть_ритейлер` with a reading

  per-comment attribution; two comments called `плюс_спам`.
- **@tarilka_malyuka:715** — a recipe thread the manual reading never touched, and the reader
  resolved **three dairy trade marks** out of it — «агуня», «мілупа», «філадельфія» — each as
  `молочный_бренд`, each with a one-phrase reading and the quote it was read in, and then a
  `похвала` signal comparing the thickness of two of them. That is duty (2) doing exactly what the
  operator's clarification of 15.08 asks for.

Two shape defects, both frozen and both reported rather than fixed:

1. **`entities` comes back as an OBJECT keyed by name**, where the schema asks for a list. All three
   replies, every other field right. The prompt's line reads «"entities" — one object per name of
   duty (2): {…}», and «one object per name» is exactly how the model rendered it.
2. **`evidence: [null]`** on the third thread's signal: the reader read the signal in the POST, and
   the prompt's own rule «the post has none: for the post the id is null» is what it applied — to a
   field the schema requires to be msg_ids. That reply still refuses after the first defect is
   coerced away, and it is the only one that does.

## 6. Findings

1. **The gate's hygiene rule is a payment gate for the reader.** Plan §2 C says the marker rule r1
   is «временная гигиена детерминированной витрины … не закон смысла; финальную атрибуцию делает
   читатель». In the censused gate it is a SILENCER, and it removes exactly the three threads whose
   entity resolution the reference makes obligatory (E1, E4a, E4b) plus one noise thread (N3). The
   reader cannot make the attribution the plan reserves for it on a thread the rule removed before
   payment. This is a design question for the sitting, not a defect in either file.
2. **The reference's quotes are a reading aid.** Six of eleven cannot be grepped in the evidence
   store, four of them on flagships. Any later gold, screen or drill-down that quotes the reference
   verbatim will be quoting something no message contains.
3. **The N list and the S list disagree about #10529, and the store settles it** — two comments, one
   of which the S list itself reads as a signal. Bar 3 cannot score both readings.
4. **plan §3 carries a subject word its own class list does not.** Its schema example uses
   `subject_type: "категория"` while the class list two paragraphs below names four; three of the
   five flagships are category-level and a reader held to the four could not report them at all. The
   prompt takes the union from the design authority and the registration names the gap as the
   sitting's to close.
5. **The two ceilings of bar 5 are not both binding, and the cheaper one is not the time one.** 30
   minutes billed is $0.5520 at the measured rate — 2.76× the $0.20 the contract wrote. The cap
   binds first, at 10.9 minutes. Any later reading of «≤30 min» as the operative budget will be
   reading the looser of the two — and the measured 54.8 s a thread blows through BOTH: 111 threads
   is 101 minutes.
6. **The reader works and the interface does not.** Three verdicts, three refusals, one cause: the
   model returns `entities` keyed by name instead of as a list. Two of the three parse whole once
   that one shape is coerced, and the third fails only on `evidence: [null]` — a rule the prompt
   itself taught it for a different field. Both are one-line prompt or parser decisions and both
   belong to the sitting, because the instrument froze the moment the endpoint existed.
7. **The census's interval was an honest bound and its lower end was 13× off.** «A thread call
   costs at least one comment call» is arithmetically safe and empirically useless: the reader's
   input is 2.1× a comment call's and its answer is 3–4× longer, and on an L4 it measured 54.8 s
   against 4.247. Any future projection that needs a per-thread price now has one — and it names
   the card it was measured on.

## 8. Process signals

1. **A cap divided by a measured rate is a decision, not an estimate.** The go/no-go was designed to
   collapse an 8×-wide interval; it never occurred to the contract that the interval's own floor,
   plus one measured weight load, is already over the ceiling. Dividing the cap by the rate took two
   minutes and replaced the whole paid rung.
2. **The population's list is worth more than its count.** Every finding in §2 — three unreachable
   entity cases, one unreachable noise thread, a msg-id under another post, a thread in two lists at
   once — came from enumerating what the census had only counted, and every one of them would
   otherwise have surfaced as a bar failing after the money was spent.
3. **A prompt registry is a pinned artefact.** Adding one prompt reddened five tests across three
   records that have nothing to do with prompts. The house manoeuvre exists and worked, but the cost
   of a fifth instrument is now visibly three pin decisions, not one edit.
4. **The preflight earns its keep on the template, not on the guards.** Nine of its ten new checks
   are pure and could have run anywhere; the tenth — that Gemma 4's own template at the pinned
   revision opens with `<bos>` and closes the thought channel on THIS request — is the one that
   could only be bought here, and it is the one that would have cost a boot to discover.
5. **The gate did its job at a sixth of the cap, and the pre-run arithmetic was right for the wrong
   reason.** §5.1 bounded the setup at $0.16 from a sibling ledger and it came in at ~$0.025; what
   blew the budget was the one term no record could price, the reading itself, at 12.9× the only
   bound that existed. The conclusion held either way — but the lesson is to name which term of a
   bound is unmeasured and buy the smallest measurement that settles THAT one, which is exactly
   what a registered warm-up is for.
