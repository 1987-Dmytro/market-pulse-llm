# 5c2 closes: the sitting ratified both halves, 3.19 rules on the text-less comments, and the shelf-life redesign is named as a house pattern

**Context:** SPEC 3.18 (6) made an operator sitting the thing phase 5c2 does not close without — «at
least five leaflet posts and five comments, ORIGINAL beside VERDICT, drawn under a recorded seed».
The pack was built at 5c2-validate-prep (`results/validate_5c2_pack.json`, sha `2fad5339…`, seed
42) and shipped with every `findings` slot empty. The sitting was held **2026-08-14**,
asynchronously: the operator read the HTML rendering and his verdicts were relayed through the team
lead. **The home of every value below is the artifact named beside it**; where the two disagree,
the artifact is the contract.

`results/validate_5c2_returns.json`, the committed copy of an evening nobody will sit through

| | disputed **0** · REVIEW-class orders issued **0** | |

The operator's words on the leaflet half, kept as data and untranslated: **«распознавание SKU
идеальное»**. `sitting.operator_minutes` is **null** — no actual was relayed and nothing on disk
carries one. `docs/STATUS.md` named ~45 minutes BEFORE the sitting; a plan is not a measurement.

What ratification does and does not buy: 3.18 (6)(c) files these verdicts in the REVIEW class of
3.16 (1). They become watchlist, lexicon or prompt-revision ORDERS and they never move a bar, a
denominator or a scored number. Zero orders means the position instrument's output survived an
examination it could have failed — it does not mean any FAIL of the sku-b program was reversed.
Bar 2 (price pairs, 0.4125) is still failed and 3.18 (2) still admits it to no price surface.

## 2. Amendment 3.19 — the one RULING the sitting produced

Referenced, never restated: the text is in `docs/SPEC.md` inside `<!-- amendment-3.19 -->`. It
answers **Dv324** — 1 361 of the 5 075 bought comment rows (26.8%) reached the model with empty
text, stickers and photos and voice notes, every one of them empty at the SOURCE store. In one
line: text-less comments leave the inference queue **from the next paid cycle**, reporting
denominators follow the rule, and nothing this run bought is re-scored.

Two consequences that belong to whoever writes the next cycle's prep:

* the exclusion is a QUEUE rule, never a deletion — the rows stay collected, watermarked and
  counted, and a producer that dropped them would have made a queue rule into data loss;
* «the volume of wordless reactions is itself a signal» — so it is printed as its own named class
  beside every distribution over comments, not blended into one.

The block landed the way every block before it has: one name short of the enumerations that hash
the file. It reddened SIX tests, all of them designed to go red for exactly this, and greening them
is [[the-law-grows-inside-marked-blocks]] applied for the fourth time. **The keep did not grow** —
`write_prereg_5c2.KEEP_BLOCKS` stays at TEN and `BLOCKS_TODAY` is the eleven the file carries now,
because one constant answering both questions would have made greening the producer re-pin a
sealed record.

## 3. The shelf-life redesign — the pattern, named once

Nine of the program's 45 tagged deviations are `[process]`, and the biggest cluster inside it is
one shape: **a green assertion whose truth was fixed to a moment, going red because the world moved
legitimately.** The wrong fix is to delete the assertion. The right one is to replace it with the
weaker statement that is *still a statement*. Four instances now, in four different subsystems:

| form | was | became |
|---|---|---|
| **freeze the constant** (cap-in-force) | `PHASE_CAP_USD`, live, 25 → 30 → 33 | `repair_phase4_ledger.CAP_IN_FORCE_USD = 25.00`, fixed to the moment its three entries were billed — records under an earlier cap are never re-scored (3.18 (7)(b)) |
| **snapshot instead of absence** (derived root) | «`data/derived/` does not exist», which 5c2-run legitimately ended | a before/after snapshot of every file and its sha, so a smoke is still refused the real root and the guard still fails on a single row |
| **strip instead of identity** (ten-keep) | «the pin equals sha256 of `docs/SPEC.md`» | «the pin equals the strip of today's file», with `live != pin` asserted beside it so a law that never grew would also fail |
| **recover instead of re-pin** (sealing commit) | «`producer.sha256` equals the live producer's bytes» | «those bytes are still fetchable at `git show 0e390ff:`, and the live file has MOVED» |

The rule the next flip cites instead of rediscovering: **when a guard reddens because something
legitimate changed, ask what invariant the guard was really protecting and assert THAT — with the
old failure planted in the other direction, so the redesign is not a relaxation.** Every one of the
four keeps a negative control: a moved KEPT block, a smoke that writes one row, a twelfth marked
block, a moved pin.

## 4. Two lines the next cycle's contracts inherit

From the `[contract-gap]` cluster — 11 of 45, the largest tag in the program:

1. **A fake must model the transport's LIMITS, not only its answers.** Dv309: the leaflet leg's
   first pack put 126 base64 pages in one body, RunPod refused it over the 10 MiB ceiling with an
   HTTP 400 before any worker saw it, and the boot was already paid for. Every stub in the suite
   modelled what the worker would REPLY; none of them modelled what the wire would refuse.
2. **A verify-gate number is re-measured when its artifact moves.** Dv281 / Dv293 / the c3a session
   header, three instances in one day: a determinism pair, a producer sha and a session line, each
   a true statement about a moment left standing after the moment moved. The re-measurement belongs
   to the gate, not to a later reader's correction.

## 5. What the phase spent, and what it leaves open

Phase 4 stands at **$31.4493 of the $33.00 cap, $1.5507 remaining**; 5c2-run bought the whole
registered window (5 075 comments + 159 leaflet pages + 44 posts, 5 423 evidence rows, zero
incomplete) for **$7.5309** of an $8.00 session cap, and every $0 contract since has cost $0.

Four debts survive the close, each with an owner:

* **the empty-comment queue rule** — 3.19 (1), implemented by the next cycle's prep, not by a
  re-scoring of what is bought;
* **leaflet collection beyond ATB** — authorised as a separate $0 task (3.18 (7)(d));
* **the 11 143 comments under the watermark** — a visibly deferred decision (3.18 (4)); entry is
  free and re-purchases were measured at zero, buying the history is its own contract;
* **the leaflet reference price 10.408 s/page over n = 159** — the number the next registration
  starts from, with older records never re-scored.
