# `guard --until` — the tolerance did not re-derive because the table measured its own window bug, and the operator closed three by ruling

**The record debt of an accepted step.** `guard-until` was executed and ACCEPTED on the evening of
2026-08-18 (`docs/STATUS.md`, «`guard-until` исполнен и ПРИНЯТ 18.08»). The argument, every command
and every figure are in `docs/reports/guard-until.md` (Dv500–Dv517) and are **not re-argued here**.
This record states what was decided.

## The finding: an artifact that could not yield the constant it was cited for

The contract's step-1 refusal gate asked for a close tolerance re-derived as the maximum divergence
of `pass1-probe`'s published control table, expecting **6–7%**. Re-derived: **3 of 7 rows inside
7%, maximum divergence 100.00%**. The useful half was not the number — every outlier decomposed
into a defect in how the table's own window was built, and none of them was billing spread:

| mechanism | measured |
|---|---|
| the window START is each ledger's first **session**, not its anchor — none of the seven carries `anchored_at`, so spend between anchor and first reading is structurally invisible | pre-window spend at 0.00 / 0.84 / 4.76 / 10.01 / 12.16 / 99.97% of each recorded reading — **and the three rows inside 7% are exactly the three with the smallest** |
| `probe_a`'s only session is written **after** the step finished, so start = end | a zero-width window; −100% is arithmetic on an empty walk |
| `5c1_vis` holds **two anchors in one file** | the walk spans two steps against a one-step reading; the row is unreachable by the instrument at all |
| the right-hand side is a **lagged reading of the same walk** | `pass1-probe-b`'s $0.0853189556 is `0.08531895559281111` — the same bounded walk truncated at 09:52:00, seven minutes earlier. The balance settles behind the bill; the ledger recorded **64.9%** of the truth |

## What was decided

1. **The gate fired and the contract closed NOTHING.** A tolerance is a claim about how far two
   independent instruments may sit apart. Where the divergence is generated by one side's window
   construction, a larger tolerance does not absorb noise — it licenses a known bug and the gate
   stops being able to refuse anything ([[a-spread-that-is-really-a-window]]).
2. **`--tolerance` ships as a REQUIRED parameter with no default.** The honest instrument makes the
   caller say the number; no figure derived from that table is registered anywhere in the code.
3. **The guard learns an end bound.** `--until` and `--expect-ms` land, the walk becomes
   three-state (readable / partial / complete) against a `MS_BAND` of 1% with a 1 000 ms floor, and
   a live close **re-walks** when a window is supplied — the one behaviour change beyond adding a
   parameter (Dv508).
4. **A step's close rests on the billing walk ALONE, and this is now written down.** `own_resources`
   excludes the always-on kinds by design; on an old ledger the balance delta is *negative* (−$4.19
   for `srv2b`, −$5.34 for `srv2d`, the account having been topped up twice since 2026-08-08), so
   «both readings and the pessimistic one binds» is structurally unavailable at close time and the
   delta sits in the entry as provenance a reader must not add up (Dv517,
   [[a-balance-delta-is-not-a-per-leg-cost]]).
5. **The preflight becomes an instrument** — `make preflight`, a pin registry of 1 466 paths across
   three pin shapes. It caught itself twice and found the guard already stale against its own pin
   before the guard was opened, which is what made the MOVED manoeuvre the right answer instead of
   a re-pin (Dv509, Dv513).

**The caveat rides INSIDE the settled records, not in a report.** Each window starts at its
ledger's first session, so each figure is right about the window it names and low about the step by
the pre-window spend — **$0.4204 · $0.0000 · $0.0104**. A closing entry is never re-derived, so the
next reader of `spend_45h2.json` learns from the entry itself that $8.217558 is a window's figure
([[evidence_artifact_cannot_be_rederived]]). Each entry also carries `window_start`,
`window_end`, `walk_ms`, `recorded_reading_usd` and the `tolerance` it was judged under, so the
verdict re-derives from the record. Append-only was proved on all four files the closes touched —
the three steps and the live cycle-2 line that witnesses each of them — and the suite after the
writes was 2 927 / 2.

**19 of 22 refuse and stay refused.** An anchor that does not exist and a window that was never
recorded are not things a band can fix.
