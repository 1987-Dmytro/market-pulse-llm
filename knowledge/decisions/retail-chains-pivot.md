# Observation points: retail chains, not producers

**Context:** the original plan observed producer brand channels. Ukrainian dairy producers barely
use Telegram for consumer communication, so those channels carry no audience reactions.

**Decision (operator, SPEC rev. 3):** observation points switch to retail-chain channels
(АТБ, Сільпо, Varus) plus discount aggregators, tagged separately in provenance because the
aggregator audience is deal-hunter biased.

**Numbers:** the registry holds 4 verified sources after the Phase 2 entry check — 3
`official_retail` (silpo, atb, varus) and 1 `aggregator` (msuaaaa). Only 2 of them have comments
enabled (varus, msuaaaa); the other two still supply launch posts for T2.

**Why:** the chains run live channels with real traffic and comment threads; the producers do not.

**Alternatives rejected:** producer channels as primary observation points · retail chains as a
secondary source only.
