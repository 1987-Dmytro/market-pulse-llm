## Findings for Phase 5 (none gate-relevant)

1. `planned` over-counts steps by one per epoch — 272 planned, **270 run** — because the epoch's
   micro-batches do not divide by the accumulation.
2. Those leftover micro-batches' gradients are never zeroed, so epoch 1's first step folds in 6 of
   epoch 0's. One step in 270 with a 1.75× effective batch.
3. The post-loop `save()` records the loop variable rather than the stop position after an early
   `--max-steps` (`epoch 1 row 0` instead of `epoch 0 row ~160`). Unreachable by a full arm; the
   in-loop `save_every` write is the same `save()` from a correct call site.
4. `seconds_per_step` in a **resumed** provenance is understated — this process's clock over the
   cumulative counter (15.8 s against a real ~43 s).
5. **Training is not bit-reproducible on this stack**: 0.18010 vs 0.18051 at step 5 on identical
   data, seed and config on the same pod. Cause not isolated (NF4 reduction order, or the LoRA
   dropout mask sequence). Say "paired on data and config", never "identical".
6. The eval's last-line crash, above.
