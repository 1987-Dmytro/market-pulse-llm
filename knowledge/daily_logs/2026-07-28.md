threads: 7,224 with text → 4,424 unlabelled → 1,068 outside those threads → **971 candidates**,

**Operator chose option 1a (hybrid) on 2026-07-28.** Then: all 971 rows labelled under the v2
calibration in chunks of 100, validator green after each — **54 sarcastic scoreable rows,
5.6% of the pool** against the 15–25% the task expected, and 31% `unclear` (support voice,
replies to other commenters, fragments). That yield is the reusable finding: it is what the
tail of an already-mined corpus looks like.

**`data/frozen/sarcasm_holdout.jsonl` frozen at 108 rows** (54 fresh + 54 moved out of the
mined pool, which drops 800 → 746). The binding constraint was never the labelling: 131 of the
187 sarcastic mined rows share a thread with a scoreable train row and cannot move. Training
keeps 230 sarcastic scoreable rows. SPEC → rev. 3.2, G1b now scores the base model on the
holdout and the slice is whatever it gets wrong — smaller than 108, unknown until Phase 3.

from the data by a scratchpad checker.

and the freeze **shrank `sarcasm_candidates.pristine.jsonl` 800 → 746** to keep the validator
green, which is legitimate here (the 54 dropped ids are exactly the 54 moved) but is also a
way to silence the check, so it went into Footguns.

`tfidf-logreg` scored through the scorer into `results/baselines.json` (append-only, read it with
`scripts/show_results.py`): G1a 0.6834 overall · ua 0.6982 · ru 0.6730 — G1c 0.6000 — G1d

amendment 3.2 defines its slice by the *zero-shot base LLM's* errors, so it belongs to 3b/4; this
baseline's own 26/108 holdout errors are recorded as a diagnostic and labelled as not-the-slice.

decimals; the run is deterministic and the committed tree reproduces it.

**G1d is two heads, and that needs an operator decision.** SPEC §5 says "relevance + 3-class",
the docstring says the 3-class post type alone. Merging them would put an n=1 class
(relevant + other) into a macro denominator and let one row swing the gate ~25 pp, so both are
reported with their own n and the choice is deferred to Phase 4.
